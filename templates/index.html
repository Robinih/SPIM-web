Features

Everything you need to monitor pests.

From instant AI scanning to municipal-wide heatmaps, SPIM gives farmers and administrators the tools to act fast.

AI Image Scanning

Point your camera at the insect. SPIM's YOLOv8s model instantly detects and classifies the species with high accuracy — even on sticky traps.

Live Heatmaps

See pest density across your municipality in real-time. Color-coded overlays show exactly which barangays are experiencing outbreaks.

Push Notifications

Receive automated alerts the moment pest thresholds are crossed in your area. Powered by Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Pest Library

Access detailed information on all 11 tracked pest species — including life cycles, damage descriptions, and recommended control methods.

Analytics Dashboard

View daily trends, species breakdowns, and historical data through interactive charts on the web dashboard.

Cloud Sync

Every scan and detection is automatically synced to the central server, building a municipal-wide dataset for smarter monitoring.

How It Works

From scan to safe,
in four steps.

The entire workflow takes less than a minute — from capturing an image to viewing your contribution on the municipal heatmap.

1

Capture the pest

Open the app and use the camera or gallery. Our AI model processes the image on your device in seconds.

2

Get instant results

SPIM identifies the species, shows confidence scores, and highlights detections with bounding boxes.

3

Sync to the cloud

Your detection is automatically uploaded and geo-tagged to your barangay, building a live map of pest activity.

4

Monitor & respond

Administrators see heatmaps and trends. Farmers get push alerts when thresholds are crossed nearby.

About

Built for the farming communities
of Cavite.

SPIM is a thesis project developed by CVSu AgriTech to modernize agricultural pest monitoring. Our early outreach included collaboration with the farming communities of Naic, Cavite.

The Problem

Manual scouting is slow and unreliable

Farmers rely on experience and guesswork to determine pest threats, leading to delayed responses, crop damage, and economic loss.

The Solution

Edge AI meets community monitoring

SPIM deploys YOLOv8s directly on-device for real-time pest detection, paired with cloud-synced municipal logging for a complete threat picture.
