Getting Started

Follow these steps to create your account and start using the SPIM mobile application to detect and track agricultural pests.

1
Download & Install

Download the latest .apk file using the button above or from our releases page. Install it on your Android device. You may need to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your device settings.

2
Create an Account

Open the app, tap Register, and fill in your details including your municipality and barangay. This helps us generate accurate heatmaps for pest monitoring in your area.

3
Log In & Sync

Log in with your credentials. Your app will automatically fetch the latest data, models, and notification configurations from the cloud server.

Scan Methods & Guidelines

The Scan feature uses advanced YOLOv8s AI models running on-device to detect and count 11 different families of rice pests.

How to Scan

  • Open the app and tap Identify (single insect) or Count (multiple insects / sticky trap).
  • Use the camera to capture a clear image, or select one from your gallery.
  • Review the detection boxes and confidence scores before saving the result.

📐 Tips for Accurate Results

  • Lighting: Ensure the subject is well-lit. Avoid harsh shadows or glare on sticky traps.
  • Distance: Keep the camera 15–30 cm away for optimal focus on smaller insects.
  • Angle: Hold the camera parallel (flat) to the surface being scanned.
  • Focus: Tap your screen to lock focus directly on the pest before capturing.

Dashboard & Monitoring

The web dashboard provides a comprehensive view of pest activity across different regions.

  • Heatmap: A visual map of pest density by barangay. Colors range from Yellow (Low) → Orange (Medium) → Red (High) based on configurable thresholds.
  • Analytics: Interactive charts showing daily pest detection trends and breakdowns by insect species.
  • Automated Alerts: Push notifications are triggered automatically when pest counts exceed thresholds. Alert levels: Low, Medium, and High severity.

Roles & Permissions

The SPIM platform supports different user roles with specific permissions:

Role Permissions
Farmer Use the mobile app to scan & identify insects, view personal detection records, access the pest library, and receive municipal push alerts.
Admin Access the web dashboard heatmap, view aggregated municipal statistics, manage farmer accounts, and configure alert thresholds.
Developer Access advanced debug views, manage all user accounts, configure system thresholds, and push application updates.
